Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

AFT Score Calculator ("the app") is an Army Fitness Test score calculator run by Savidge Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. You can reach us at savidgeapps@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The calculators work without an account, and nothing you enter in them is sent to us.
  • If you create an account, we store your sign-in details and the scores you choose to save.
  • Your saved scores feed anonymous percentile comparisons. Other people never see your name, email or individual scores.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, set to non-personalized.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and saved scores inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Without an account. The calculators, goal planner and body-composition tools run on your phone. The numbers you type stay on your phone.

With an account. When you register we store a username, your email address, your first and last name, and a password (kept only as a salted hash, never in readable form). You can also give your gender and whether you are in a combat MOS, which the scoring standards use.

Saved scores. When you tap Save score we store the date, your age, the standard (AFT or ACFT), the result and score for each event, and your total score, linked to your account.

Signing in. The app keeps a sign-in token in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). The server records when it was last used and ends it after 90 days without use, when you sign out, and when your password changes.

Password reset. If you ask to reset your password we email a six-digit code to your account's address. We store only a scrambled version of the code, and it stops working after 15 minutes or when it is used.

Username reminder. If you ask for your username we email it to your account's address. We do not store anything extra for this, and the app never says whether an address has an account.

Error reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, it can send a short anonymous report to Sentry so we can fix the problem. A report has the error, which part of the app it happened in, the app version, your phone's operating system and model, and a short trail of recent actions with ids and addresses removed. It does not include your name, username, email address, IP address, scores, passwords or anything you typed, and no screenshots are taken. Reports are kept only for a limited time.

Technical data. Our server may log standard request details such as your IP address and the time, which we use to keep the service running and to limit abuse.

How we use it

  • To run your account, show your saved scores, and let you reset your password.
  • To work out percentiles: we compare your total with other users' saved results in the same group (the same age bracket, the same scoring column for male or combat MOS, and the same combat-MOS setting; only each person's latest result counts). A percentile is shown only when the group has at least 20 results, so a small group can never single out one person. Other people's results are never shown to you either.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Who else sees data

  • Heroku hosts our server and database in the United States.
  • Google (Gmail) sends the password-reset emails, so it handles your email address and the code.
  • Google AdMob shows the banner ad. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and device and app details to serve and measure ads. We ask AdMob for non-personalized ads only. On iPhone the app asks permission for tracking; if you decline, the ad identifier is not used.
  • Sentry receives the anonymous error reports described above and stores them in the United States.
  • Expo delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update, Expo's servers receive your app version, platform and IP address.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and saved scores until you delete them. You can delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account), which removes your account details and all your saved scores straight away. You can also ask by email. See how to delete your account. Database backups kept by our hosting provider may hold deleted data for up to 90 days before they are replaced.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sign-in, registration and password-reset attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can change your details and password in the app, delete your account, and decline the iPhone tracking prompt. You can ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU and UK) you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

Not an official app

The app is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the U.S. Army or the Department of Defense.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
