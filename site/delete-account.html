Delete your AFT Score Calculator account

App: AFT Score Calculator by Savidge Apps.

Delete it in the app

1. Open AFT Score Calculator and sign in.

2. Go to User Account (Profile).

3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

4. Your account and saved scores are deleted straight away and you are signed out.

If you cannot use the app

Email savidgeapps@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my AFT Score Calculator account". We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username, email address, first and last name, and password.
  • Your optional details: gender and combat-MOS setting.
  • All your saved scores and results.
  • Your sign-in tokens and any pending password-reset code.

What stays

  • Nothing that identifies you stays on our live database.
  • Database backups kept by our hosting provider can hold your data for up to 90 days after deletion, until they are replaced. They are not used for anything else.

Anything you entered in the calculators without an account is only on your phone. Delete the app or clear its data to remove it.

Questions

Email savidgeapps@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
